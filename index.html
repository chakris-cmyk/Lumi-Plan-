03 · Social content engine

Three reels per week built around practical product value

Create 10–30 second vertical videos that show real Luma use cases. Adapt the hook, caption and CTA for each platform.

Reel 1 · Product demonstration

Show the product working

Screen-recorded walkthroughs of duplicate or similar photo discovery, review controls and the actual cleanup flow.

Creative angle: “Your iPhone may be storing more duplicate photos than you realise.”

Story arc: recognisable situation → live app demonstration → visible, truthful result → App Store CTA.

Reel 2 · Problem and solution

Start with a familiar frustration

Open with the “iPhone storage full” moment, then demonstrate how Luma supports reviewing photos and videos before anything is removed.

Creative angle: “Before deleting your favourite photos, review what is taking up space.”

Story arc: problem → consequence → Luma workflow → user-controlled next step.

Reel 3 · Feature, education or trust

Explain the value behind the feature

Highlight privacy, user control, a specific feature or a genuine Storage Wrapped result. Claims must match the live product experience.

Creative angle: “A cleaner app should leave the final decision in your hands.”

Story arc: question or misconception → concise explanation → product proof → low-friction CTA.

The weekly cadence yields approximately 39 core reels over 13 publishing weeks. The initial setup period establishes the creative system; the weekly rhythm continues throughout the 90-day programme.

Creative and storytelling standards

  • Place the product or the core problem within the first 2–3 seconds.
  • Use a single message and one clear action per reel.
  • Add clear subtitles for sound-off viewing.
  • Show real app screens and results; avoid fake system alerts.
  • Test alternate opening hooks before rebuilding a complete concept.
  • Refresh ads when watch time or engagement drops.

Creative evaluation

  • Early retention and three-second hold rate.
  • Average watch time and completion rate.
  • Shares and saves as indicators of usefulness.
  • Profile visits, link clicks and store-page visits.
  • For paid creatives: CPI and post-install activation by creative.

Judge reach creatives and install creatives separately. Views alone do not prove acquisition value.

04 · Meta paid strategy

Two separate new campaigns with distinct optimisation goals

Both campaigns are new proposals: one drives app installs; the other builds reel reach and engagement.

Proposed new campaign 1

Meta App Install Campaign

₹300/day

Objective: App Promotion / App Installs.

  • Destination: Luma's Apple App Store listing for iOS.
  • Markets: prioritised Tier 1 markets, with eligible Tier 2 test markets considered within a consolidated initial structure; exclude the EU. India remains organic-first and TikTok-excluded.
  • Creative supply: two to three reel-based variations covering product demo, problem/solution and user control.
  • Audience: broad iPhone-eligible audiences, subject to available platform controls and app eligibility.
  • Measurement: spend, attributed installs, CPI, country, creative, store-page conversion and post-install activation/retention.

Country-level reporting should be enabled without fragmenting the ₹300 daily budget into numerous separately funded ad sets.

Proposed new campaign 2

Meta Reel Performance Campaign

₹200/day

Objective: Video Views when watch time and view volume are the primary goals; Engagement when meaningful interactions are the primary goal.

  • Creative supply: the three weekly reels, with rotation based on performance.
  • Optimisation: watch time, completion rate and engagement response.
  • Measurement: reach, video views, CPM, cost per view, saves, shares and profile visits.
  • Role: build awareness, creative learning and audience familiarity; it is not the app-install acquisition campaign.

Choose one objective at launch; do not mix awareness and install targets.

05 · TikTok paid acquisition

One App Promotion campaign, budgeted independently of Meta

Organic TikTok is included in the weekly content plan. Paid TikTok needs a separate, substantially larger budget.

One proposed campaign

TikTok App Promotion · iOS installs

  • Campaign count: one App Promotion campaign optimised for App Installs.
  • Destination: Luma's iOS App Store listing.
  • Markets: eligible priority markets from the source plan—US, Canada and UK first; UAE and Saudi Arabia only after country-level Ads Manager eligibility checks. India is excluded. Australia is deferred pending a market review.
  • Ad-group structure: market groupings within the single campaign, consolidated where minimum budgets and delivery allow. Avoid multiplying low-volume ad groups.
  • Creative testing: the same three weekly reels, adapted with TikTok-native pacing, opening text and captions.
  • Measurement: installs, CPI, country/ad group, creative, app event measurement, activation and retention.
Official published budget threshold
US$50+/day

Campaign-level daily budget must exceed US$50. Each daily-budget ad group must exceed US$20.

Indicative conversion: US$1 = ₹93.46 (planning assumption, not a live executable FX quote).

Campaign threshold: more than ₹4,673/day.

Ad-group threshold: more than ₹1,869/day for each daily-budget ad group.

Ad-group minimums are additional to the campaign minimum, so the actual total may be higher.

Budget decision: TikTok is not included in the ₹500/day Meta allocation. The lean launch path is the two-campaign Meta pilot plus organic TikTok distribution. Paid TikTok acquisition requires a separate incremental budget above the verified campaign minimum, and should proceed only after country eligibility, iOS attribution and the intended ad-group configuration are confirmed in the live account.

TikTok's official July 2026 Ads Manager budget guidance states that campaign daily budgets must exceed US$50 and ad-group daily budgets must exceed US$20. Thresholds may vary with account setup, market, currency and Ads Manager configuration; the live account is authoritative at launch.

06 · Organic distribution

Platform-native distribution across seven channels

Repurpose the weekly videos across short-form platforms. Use community and editorial channels to answer real questions, not to repeatedly promote the app.

Instagram Reels

Primary video channel
  • Publish three reels per week on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
  • Use a distinct first-line hook, concise caption and direct App Store or profile CTA.
  • Use stories to support new reels, feature explanations and product updates.
  • Review retention, shares, saves, profile visits and link activity weekly.

YouTube Shorts

Search and discovery
  • Republish all three weekly vertical videos.
  • Use platform-specific titles and descriptions that clearly describe the iPhone storage problem or feature.
  • Keep the first seconds visually clear and avoid Instagram watermarks.
  • Measure views, average view duration, viewed-versus-swiped behaviour and channel visits.

TikTok · Organic

Native short-form reach
  • Publish the same three core videos each week with TikTok-native opening text, pacing and captions.
  • Use native sounds only where appropriate and rights permit.
  • Use comments to identify recurring storage questions and future reel topics.
  • Keep organic distribution active independently of the paid campaign decision.

Reddit

Community participation
  • Participate in relevant discussions about iPhone storage, photo/video management, duplicate photos, storage optimisation and iOS utilities.
  • Prioritise useful answers, such as reviewing large videos, checking duplicates and backing up important items before deletion.
  • Disclose the Luma affiliation when relevant; mention the product only where community rules allow.
  • Initial operating range: 5–8 thoughtful comments and no more than one original post per week, subject to subreddit rules.
  • Avoid repetitive posts, unsolicited links and copied replies.

Quora

Evergreen answers
  • Publish approximately two useful answers per week.
  • Focus on iPhone storage-full questions, duplicate photos, video cleanup and iCloud storage decisions.
  • Make every answer useful on its own.
  • Disclose the Luma connection; mention the app only when relevant.
  • Measure answer views, engagement, referral visits and attributable installs where available.

Substack

Editorial credibility
  • Publish one editorial article every two weeks.
  • Potential themes: a practical iPhone storage reset, reviewing duplicate photos safely and what to consider before buying more storage.
  • Use original screenshots and a transparent developer note.
  • Repurpose article insights into short social posts and relevant conversations.

X

Founder- or brand-led presence
  • Maintain a consistent presence through product updates, useful observations, short clips and relevant conversations.
  • Target one substantive post per day and 3–5 thoughtful replies to relevant conversations, subject to capacity.
  • Use build-in-public observations and product learnings rather than fabricated user stories or engagement bait.
  • Grok references or tags remain occasional and contextually relevant, not repetitive reach tactics.
  • Measure meaningful replies, reposts, profile visits, link clicks and attributable installs—not impressions alone.
Community integrity: Reddit, Quora, Substack and X participation is governed by platform and community rules. Brand affiliation should be transparent. Repetitive promotional posts, unsolicited links, bulk mentions and engagement manipulation are excluded from the operating approach.

07 · 90-day execution roadmap

19 November 2026 – 16 February 2027

The plan moves from setup to a Meta pilot, optimisation and a 90-day investment decision. TikTok paid launch depends on budget and eligibility.

19–25 Nov 2026Phase 1 · Strategy and setup

Establish the operating foundation

  • Confirm the three-reel content pillars, scripts, creative templates and weekly production workflow.
  • Configure the proposed Meta App Install and Reel Performance campaign structures.
  • Verify the App Store destination, iOS attribution, app events and measurement access.
  • Set up organic publishing profiles and a weekly performance scorecard.
  • Confirm priority market eligibility and exclusions across platforms.
26 Nov–2 Dec 2026Phase 2 · Initial content and baseline

Begin publishing and establish benchmarks

  • Start the three-reel weekly publishing cadence across Instagram, YouTube Shorts and organic TikTok.
  • Establish baseline retention, watch time, completion, saves, shares and profile visits.
  • Prepare two to three Meta ad creative variations from the core reels.
  • Validate app-install event measurement and store destination before paid launch.
  • Begin Reddit participation, two Quora answers per week, Substack editorial cadence and consistent X activity.
3–16 Dec 2026Phase 3 · Meta pilot launch

Launch both proposed Meta campaigns

  • Launch the new Meta App Install campaign at the proposed ₹300/day allocation.
  • Launch the separate Meta Reel Performance campaign at the proposed ₹200/day allocation.
  • Test product demonstration, problem/solution and feature/trust creative variations.
  • Establish country-level reporting without excessive ad-set fragmentation.
  • Review delivery, CPI, reach, video views and early post-install signals.
17–31 Dec 2026Phase 4 · Creative optimisation

Identify stronger hooks and product demonstrations

  • Compare watch time, completion rate, engagement and CPI by creative.
  • Refresh openings and captions for stronger concepts; retain the underlying product proof.
  • Review market-level install quality and store-page conversion.
  • Evaluate TikTok's live campaign minimum, market eligibility and attribution feasibility before any paid commitment.
  • Maintain three reels per week and the organic community programme.
1–11 Jan 2027Phase 5 · Market and relaunch preparation

Prepare the New Year storage-use-case moment

  • Develop a “New Year iPhone storage reset” content sequence.
  • Prepare fresh openings for the strongest reel concepts.
  • Review App Store listing clarity, conversion and product readiness.
  • Coordinate founder-led updates and community participation within platform rules.
  • Maintain spend discipline until install quality and attribution are sufficiently reliable.
12–25 Jan 2027Phase 6 · Coordinated relaunch

Use the source-plan relaunch moment

  • Use 12 January as the coordinated Luma relaunch date identified in the source growth plan.
  • Publish a launch reel, feature demonstrations and founder/product story across short-video channels.
  • Support the launch through X and relevant community discussions where permitted.
  • Use paid creative variants that have already shown meaningful response.
  • Increase spend only when CPI and install quality meet agreed thresholds.
26 Jan–8 Feb 2027Phase 7 · Quality-led optimisation

Concentrate effort on stronger markets and creative

  • Compare country and creative combinations on install quality, not reach alone.
  • Test a new creative angle against the strongest existing concept.
  • Review D1/D7 retention and first meaningful app action where available.
  • Pause low-quality install sources, even if CPI is cheap.
  • Continue the weekly content cadence and organic distribution programme.
9–16 Feb 2027Phase 8 · 90-day review

Make the next-quarter investment decision

  • Compare organic and paid discovery, installs, CPI, store-page conversion and retention.
  • Rank markets and creative concepts by acquisition quality and cost.
  • Decide whether each paid campaign should scale, hold or stop.
  • Revisit paid payback modelling once Pro timing, pricing and conversion data are confirmed.
  • Approve the next 90-day content themes, channel priorities and budget envelope.
